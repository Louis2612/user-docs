# Getting Started with Emphyra

Set up your Emphyra account, create your organization, and get familiar with the platform.

Welcome to Emphyra.

This guide will help you get set up and familiar with the platform. We'll start with your account and organization, then take a quick look at your Home page and profile.

By the end, you'll know where to find the tools you need to manage your organization, and exactly what to do first, second, and third.

## Set Up Your Account

Select **Sign in with Google** to access Emphyra using your Google account. There's no separate password to create or remember. Emphyra uses your existing Google credentials, so setup takes seconds and your account stays protected by Google's own security.

## Create your organization

Enter your organization name and select your country. Your country matters beyond just an address field. It determines the statutory and compliance settings available to you later, like provident fund, social security, and tax numbers under Employer Settings, as well as which public holidays and leave defaults Emphyra suggests.

When you're ready, select **Continue**.

## Complete your profile

As the first user, you'll be asked to enter your personal details: name, contact information, and basic employment details. This creates your administrator profile and gives you access to your organization's settings from day one.

Everything you enter here can be reviewed and updated later from your profile at the top of the sidebar, so there's no pressure to get every field perfect on the first pass.

## Complete Your Setup

Once setup is complete, you'll be taken to the Home page. From here, you can start setting up your organization (adding employees, defining roles, and configuring leave policies) as well as managing day-to-day workflows like leave requests and approvals.

:::tip
If you're setting up Emphyra for your organization, complete the initial setup as the primary Admin. This gives that first account full visibility while everything is being configured.
:::

You can add other users and manage their access later, once your Roles and User Groups are in place. Nothing is locked in permanently at this stage.

## Your Home Page

Your Home page is your starting point whenever you sign in to Emphyra. It gives you quick access to important actions and information without having to search through different sections. Think of it as your daily control panel rather than a static dashboard.

### Apply for Leave

Need to request time off? Use the **Leave** widget on the Home page to submit a leave request for yourself, right from where you land.

Available leave types depend on the policies configured for your organization, so what you see here will reflect whatever's been set up under Leave Settings.

If nothing shows up yet, that usually just means a leave policy hasn't been created for your organization. That's covered later in this guide.

### Your Profile

Your profile is always available at the top of the sidebar, no matter which page you're on. Select your name or email address to view and update your information.

Your profile contains four areas:

- **Personal:** Your basic personal and identity information, such as your name and date of birth.
- **Employment:** Information about your role and employment within the organization, including your position and employment history.
- **Contact:** Your phone number, email address, and residential or mailing address.
- **Emergency Contact:** Details for the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency, so this information is on hand when it's needed most.

Keeping these up to date isn't just good practice. Several of these fields feed directly into your employee record, so an accurate profile means accurate records across the organization.

## Finding Your Way Around

The sidebar is your main way of navigating Emphyra, and it stays consistent no matter where you are in the platform.

You'll find the following sections:

- **Home:** Your main dashboard, and the fastest way back to your leave widget and daily overview.
- **Employer:** Manage your organization's information and settings, from legal details to statutory information and work positions.
- **Employees:** Manage employee records, including adding people one at a time or importing them in bulk.
- **User Group:** Manage groups and their assigned users, so you can grant access to several people at once.
- **Roles:** Configure permissions and access, defining exactly what each type of user can see and do.
- **Audit:** Review changes made within your organization, so you always have a record of who did what and when.
- **Leave Approval:** Review employee leave requests as they come in.
- **Leave Settings:** Configure your organization's leave policies, including entitlements and eligibility.

Your profile stays at the top of the sidebar, while **Sign Out** is available at the bottom, always in the same place however deep you are in the platform.

:::tip
When configuring Emphyra for the first time, set up your Roles before creating User Groups. User Groups use the roles you create to determine what users can access, so having your roles ready first means you won't have to stop halfway through building a group to define one.
:::

## You're Ready to Go

That's everything you need to know to get started: your account, your organization, your Home page, and how the sidebar fits together.
